Industries: estimate revisions and relative price

Each scored stock (5+ analysts and a price) is placed in its sector and industry group. Every stock counts equally: the figures are medians, not market-cap weighted. Sectors are in the usual order; click a sector to see its groups below.

What the columns mean
  • Net EPS upgrades: share of the scored stocks whose consensus EPS rose more than 0.5% in 4 weeks, minus the share whose EPS fell more than 0.5%.
  • EPS 4W: median 4-week change in consensus average EPS (nearer fiscal year).
  • EPS 13W: median 13-week change in consensus average EPS.
  • TP 4W: median 4-week change in consensus average target price.
  • 1M vs NIFTY and 3M vs NIFTY: median of the stock's return minus NIFTY's return, as of the latest trading day.

Long ideas, 1-month horizon

Ranked every week across all scored stocks (5+ analysts and a price), so the list changes as estimates and prices move. Highest scores: consensus EPS and target raised over 4 weeks while the price has not run up in the last month, in a stock that rose over the year before.

Short ideas, 1-month horizon

Lowest scores among the F&O stocks in the same ranking (an overnight short in India needs futures or options): estimates and targets being cut, often after a price run-up, in a stock that has lagged over the year.

Divergence: estimates and price moving apart

Scored stocks where the 4-week change in consensus EPS and the 4-week price move disagree, ranked by the size of the gap.

Consensus EPS down more than 1% in 4 weeks while the price rose more than 3%: the rally may be running ahead of the numbers.

Consensus EPS up more than 1% in 4 weeks while the price fell more than 3%: the price may not have caught up with the numbers yet.
